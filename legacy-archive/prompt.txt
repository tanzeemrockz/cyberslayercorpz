### 3. Technical Engine Specifications

#### Scroll Progress Engine Formula

For each pinned section wrapper, section progress is calculated dynamically inside a `requestAnimationFrame` loop:

$$\text{Progress} = \text{clamp}\left( \frac{\text{window.scrollY} - \text{Container.offsetTop}}{\text{Container.offsetHeight} - \text{window.innerHeight}},\, 0,\, 1 \right)$$

#### Horizontal Scroll Matrix Formula

For Section 03 (Project Gallery), the vertical scroll percentage is translated into horizontal position:

$$\text{translateX} = -\left( \text{Progress} \times 50\% \right)$$

#### Responsive Fallback Rules ($< 768\text{px}$)

1. On viewports below $768\text{px}$, sticky pinning is automatically disabled via CSS media queries.
2. The layout converts into a native vertical column.
3. Anime.js `IntersectionObserver` triggers simple fade-ins upon entering the viewport instead of scrubbed pinning.
